# flood-resilient-emergency-transit-plan
Flood-Resilient Emergency Transit Plan for Gandhinagar


**Team:** KAIR for Cities  
**Team Members:** Ashwini, Kaira, Radhe and Ishani  
**Problem Statement:** Urban Flash-Flooding & Emergency Corridor Preservation

## Project Overview
This project proposes a flood-resilient emergency transit plan for the Mahatma Mandir Underpass stretch and surrounding sectors in Gandhinagar, with a focus on maintaining connectivity to Civil Hospital during heavy rainfall and urban flooding.

## Key Objectives
- Maintain emergency vehicle accessibility during flooding.
- Identify alternative emergency routes and designated Life-Line Lanes.
- Reduce surface water accumulation through low-cost urban sponge interventions.
- Improve flood-risk communication and public safety.

## Proposed Solutions
1. **Emergency Route Continuity:** Alternative routes, priority lanes and pedestrian evacuation pathways.
2. **Urban Sponge Interventions:** Rain gardens, bioswales, permeable paving and soak pits.
3. **Early Warning Systems:** Water-depth markers, warning barriers and digital hazard boards.
4. **Emergency Preparedness:** A coordinated action plan for municipal officials, emergency teams, traffic police and volunteers.

## Expected Impact
Improved emergency accessibility, reduced urban waterlogging, better hazard awareness and safer evacuation during extreme rainfall.

## Project Documentation
The PDF proposal in this repository presents the study area, vulnerability analysis, proposed interventions, emergency response strategy and implementation plan.
